




# FlowForge Course K–12 Master (Final Consolidated Version)

## Description

A production-grade Google Flow + Veo 3 framework for creating structured K–12 lesson and chapter videos using sequential 8-second Veo clips, optimized for understanding, retention, progressive learning, lesson mastery, and Flow Add-to-Prompt continuity.

The purpose is to teach a lesson or chapter thoroughly and systematically.

---

# Primary Objective

I want to create a school lesson video based on a curriculum lesson or chapter.

The goal is not merely to introduce a concept.

The goal is not merely to explain a topic.

The goal is to teach the lesson effectively so that learners can:

* Understand
* Connect ideas
* Remember
* Apply basic knowledge
* Build confidence

---

# Primary Educational Success Metric

The primary success metric is:

"Does this lesson reduce learner confusion and increase understanding?"

Every scene must contribute to:

* Clarity
* Understanding
* Retention
* Confidence

If a scene fails to do so, redesign or remove it.

# Course Philosophy

This is a LESSON video.

Do NOT create:

* A simple concept explainer
* A revision-only session
* A practice-only session

Focus on:

Understand
↓
Learn
↓
Connect
↓
Remember

Outcome:

Lesson Mastery

---

# Knowledge Scaffolding Rule

Build learning progressively.

Always move from:

Known
↓
Simple
↓
Related
↓
Complex

Never introduce advanced concepts before foundations.

---

# Confusion-First Teaching Rule

Do not organize the lesson around information.

Organize the lesson around learner confusion.

For every major concept:

Identify:

* What learners misunderstand
* Why they misunderstand it
* What explanation removes the misunderstanding

Design learning around resolving confusion.

---

# One Scene = One Learning Outcome Rule

Each scene should teach one specific piece of knowledge.

Avoid:

* Multiple major concepts
* Overloaded scenes
* Dense information

Each scene should produce one clear learning gain.

* Reinforcement ideas for parents

# Age-Appropriate Teaching Rule

* Examples
* Analogies
* Visual complexity
* Teaching depth

to the selected grade level.

Every major concept must be reinforced using at least one of:

* Example
* Analogy
* Comparison
* Visualization
* Real-life connection

# Confidence Building Rule

Every major concept should increase learner confidence.

Learners should repeatedly feel:

"I understand this now."

Avoid:

* Making concepts appear more difficult than necessary
* Unnecessary complexity
* Intimidating explanations

Prefer:

* Clarity
* Simplicity
* Progress

---

# Memory Retention Rule

Create memory anchors whenever possible.

Use:

* Visual associations
* Analogies
* Patterns
* Cause-and-effect relationships
* Real-life references

The learner should remember the lesson later.

## Section 3 — Output Structure, Quality Standards & Production Rules

---

# Lesson Summary Rule

At the end of the lesson provide:

---

## Chapter Recap

Summarize:

* What the lesson taught
* How the concepts connect
* Why the lesson matters

Use age-appropriate language.

---

## Key Learnings

List:

* Most important concepts
* Most important relationships
* Most important memory anchors

Keep only essential takeaways.

---

## Lesson Mastery Check

Create:

3 simple understanding questions.

Purpose:

* Check comprehension
* Reinforce learning
* Encourage recall

Do NOT make them difficult.

Do NOT turn this into an exam.

Do not provide answers immediately.

---

# Flow Add-to-Prompt Continuity Rules

Assume every scene begins immediately after the previous scene ends.

Maintain:

* Visual continuity
* Educational continuity
* Motion continuity
* Narration continuity
* Presenter continuity (if applicable)

Avoid:

* Hard resets
* Style changes
* Environment changes
* Unnecessary reintroductions

---

# Educational Effectiveness Rule

Every scene must contribute to at least one of:

* Understanding
* Retention
* Confidence
* Lesson Progression

If a scene contributes to none of these:

Redesign or remove it.

---

# Cognitive Load Rule

Keep information manageable.

Avoid:

* Too many concepts in one scene
* Dense terminology
* Long explanations
* Excessive visual complexity

Each scene should remain comfortably understandable within 8 seconds.

---

# Knowledge Progression Rule

The lesson must feel progressive.

Learners should feel:

"I understand this step."

before moving to the next step.

Never force learners to make large conceptual jumps.

---

# Educational Quality Standards

Target:

* Lesson Understanding
* Reduced Confusion
* Strong Retention
* Confidence Building
* Parent-Friendly Learning
* Age-Appropriate Learning
* Lesson Mastery

---

# Negative Instructions

Avoid:

* Information dumping
* Chapter summarization without teaching
* Exam cramming
* Multiple major concepts in one scene
* Excessive jargon
* Weak reinforcement
* Visual clutter
* Confusing analogies
* Abrupt transitions
* Entertainment without learning value
